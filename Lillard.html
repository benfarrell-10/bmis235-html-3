Damian Lillard

Why Damian Lillard is one of the greatest Blazers ever

The argument for Damian Lillard being the greatest player in Portland Trail Blazers history rests on his unmatched scoring dominance, legendary clutch moments, and deep cultural bond with the city across multiple chapters in a Blazers uniform. Drafted sixth overall in 2012, Lillard immediately won unanimous Rookie of the Year honors and blossomed into the franchise's all-time leading scorer—surpassing Clyde Drexler's long-standing mark—while also setting the Blazers' single-game scoring record with 71 points in 2023 and ranking near the top of franchise history in assists and three-pointers made. Beyond his seven All-NBA selections and numerous All-Star appearances, Lillard defined "Dame Time" by delivering two of the most iconic series-clinching buzzer-beaters in NBA playoff history (against Houston in 2014 and Oklahoma City in 2019) and carrying Portland to eight straight postseason appearances, including a 2019 Western Conference Finals run. Even after a brief stint in Milwaukee, his return to Portland for the 2026–27 season reinforced his status as the ultimate modern icon of Rip City—combining historic individual offensive peaks with an enduring loyalty that rivals Bill Walton's championship glory and Clyde Drexler's sustained 1990s success.
